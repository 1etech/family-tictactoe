UNO How to play

2 to 10 players · 7 cards each · first to 500 points wins

1Goal

Be the first to get rid of all your cards.

2On your turn

Play one card that matches the top card by colour, number or symbol, or play a wild.

Can't play, or don't want to? Tap the draw pile. If the card you draw fits, play it straight away or tap Keep it. If it doesn't fit, your turn ends.

3Action cards

SkipThe next player misses their turn.
ReversePlay changes direction. With 2 players it works like a Skip.
Draw TwoThe next player draws 2 cards and misses their turn.
WildPlay it on anything and choose the next colour.
Wild Draw FourChoose the colour. The next player draws 4 and misses their turn. Only allowed when you have no cards of the colour in play.

No stacking: a Draw Two can't be passed on with another Draw Two.

4Shout UNO!

Down to two cards? Tap 📣 UNO! before you play (or straight after).

Forgot? Anyone can tap 🫵 Catch before the next player moves, and you draw 2.

5Scoring

Whoever goes out first scores the cards left in everyone else's hands:

Number cardsface value
Skip, Reverse, Draw Two20 each
Wild, Wild Draw Four50 each

🏆 First to 500 points wins the game.

6Good to know

  • The first card is never a wild. If it's a Skip, Reverse or Draw Two, it counts against the first player.
  • When the draw pile runs out, the played cards are shuffled back in.
  • Someone gone quiet? After about 20 seconds you can Skip them: they draw 1 and play moves on.
  • Arrived mid-round? You'll be dealt in next round.
